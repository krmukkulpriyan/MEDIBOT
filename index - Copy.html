💡 Project Abstract

MEDIBOT solves critical delays in emergency medicine access across semi-urban and rural areas during late hours or severe weather. Combining high-torque drive motors, a heavy-duty hybrid chassis, real-time IoT tracking, and an RFID-locked compartment, MEDIBOT safely transports medical supplies directly to patient doorsteps without human intervention.

The Emergency Delivery Crisis

  • Critical Time Delay: Patients in villages, small towns, and residential colonies struggle to get emergency medicines during late-night hours.
  • Lack of Transport: Delivery services are often unavailable during severe weather, late nights, or in remote roads.
  • Transit Security: High risk of medicine theft, mishandling, or contamination during manual transit.

The MEDIBOT Solution

  • Direct Autonomous Dispatch: Travels directly from local pharmacies/dispensaries straight to patient doorsteps.
  • All-Weather & All-Terrain: Designed to navigate gravel, unpaved colony roads, and speed bumps without human assistance.
  • RFID Smart Vault: Locked compartment ensures medicines are untouched until opened by the patient's verified RFID card.

✨ Key Benefits & Social Impact

MEDIBOT offers key advantages over traditional delivery methods:

1. 24/7 Availability

Operates round-the-clock without driver fatigue, solving late-night emergency medicine access in rural and semi-urban areas.

2. Theft-Proof Protection

Servo-driven electronic lock stays secured. Compartment unlocks strictly upon scanning the customer's RFID key card.

3. All-Terrain Navigation

High-torque motors and heavy chassis conquer uneven roads, gravel paths, and colony lanes regular vehicles avoid.

4. Zero Human Contact

Eliminates viral contamination and cross-infection risks during epidemics or when delivering sensitive medical supplies.

5. Real-Time Telemetry

Both pharmacy owners and patients can view live road position, battery health, and estimated arrival time on the web platform.

6. Eco-Friendly & Low Cost

Rechargeable battery electric drive eliminates fuel expenses and carbon emissions, drastically reducing delivery costs.

🛠️ Complete Hardware Component Matrix

System components selected for durability, real-time control, and maximum cost efficiency:

Microcontroller Core

  • Arduino Mega 2560 (Main Logic)
  • ESP8266 / ESP32 (Wi-Fi/IoT)

Drivetrain & Motors

  • 12V High-Torque Geared DC Motors
  • BTS7960 / L298N Motor Drivers
  • All-Terrain Rubber Wheels

Security Vault System

  • MFRC522 RFID Reader Module
  • High-Torque Servo Latch (MG996R)
  • RFID Smart Cards / Key Fobs

Sensors & Navigation

  • HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensors
  • IR Array Sensors (Path Tracking)
  • NEO-6M GPS Module

Chassis & Structure

  • 1.0 mm Mild Steel Base Plate
  • Modular Weatherproof Body
  • Mounting Brackets & Hardware

Power & Display Interface

  • 12V Rechargeable Battery Pack
  • 16x2 I2C LCD Status Display
  • Voltage Regulators & Switches

🌐 System Architecture & Web Integration

3-Layer hardware architecture synchronized with an online portal:

1. Input Layer

  • Ultrasonic & IR Sensors (Obstacle detection)
  • MFRC522 RFID Reader (Authentication)
  • NEO-6M GPS (Coordinates)

2. Processing Core

  • Arduino Mega 2560 (Real-time motor & lock control)
  • ESP8266/ESP32 (IoT web communications)

3. Output Layer

  • BTS7960 High-Power Drivers
  • High-Torque Servo Vault Lock
  • 16x2 LCD Live Display
